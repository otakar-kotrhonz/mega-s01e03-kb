The problems

parser — the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a day-first date comes out with day and month swapped: through the app, by email and through the booking-v2 form. The queued fix is for this one.

hint — booking-v3 prints a month-first hint under its date field. Only tenants on booking-v3, and only reports through that form. People type what the hint tells them, month first; the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The result looks the same as parser, so subject-line merging put it in #4471, but the parser fix does not touch it. The fix is the hint (or reading the field the way it hints).

shift — the date lands a day early. Day and month are not swapped: the weekday moves back by one, a client in another time zone gets a different day for the same meeting, an invite arrives before the booking was made. That is a date stored or sent as a midnight time and converted across time zones. Neither code path in causes.ts produces it, so the cause table on #4471 files these under the tenant’s cause with a footnote. The table above counts them the desk’s way: where they are filed, and again as shift.

Why the merge hid them

  • Merging by subject line (decision 0001) cannot see any of this: every problem arrives as “wrong day”.
  • Intake tags come from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant. The table counts by tenant and form; compare its two columns.
  • The cause table on #4471 picks the biggest group as the ticket’s cause and marks the rest “no ticket of its own”. They have tickets now.

What to do

  • Keep #4471 for parser; the other problems have their own tickets above.
  • Ship the parser fix, and check booking-v3 and the time-zone path separately: the parser fix does not reach them.
